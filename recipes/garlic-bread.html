Garlic Bread

This irresistible cheesy garlic bread features thick slices of artisanal bread slathered in rich garlic butter, topped with melted mozzarella, and baked until bubbly and golden brown. Served fresh on a rustic wooden board, each piece offers a perfect balance of a crunchy crust and a soft, cheesy center.

Ingredients

  • Bread slices
  • Minced garlic
  • Softened butter
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh parsley
  • Salt and pepper

Steps

  • Combine the softened butter, minced garlic, a pinch of salt, and chopped parsley in a small bowl.
  • Spread the garlic butter mixture evenly over one side of each bread slice.
  • Top the buttered bread generously with a thick layer of shredded mozzarella cheese.
  • Arrange the slices on a baking sheet and bake in a preheated oven until the cheese melts.
  • Broil for the final minute until the cheese becomes bubbly and develops golden spots.
  • Transfer the hot slices to a wooden board, garnish with extra parsley, and serve immediately.
