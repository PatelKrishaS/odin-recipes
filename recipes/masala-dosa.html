Masala Dosa

Description

This authentic South Indian Masala Dosa features thin, crispy rice-and-lentil crepes filled with a flavorful, spiced potato stuffing and a signature fiery red chutney spread. Traditionally served on vibrant banana leaves, this classic dish brings a perfect blend of a shattering, golden crust and a soft, deeply savory interior.

The hot crepes are paired with a piping-hot bowl of aromatic vegetable sambar and a variety of freshly ground chutneys for dipping. A rich dollop of fresh butter melting over the top adds a luxurious finish, making this breakfast favorite an absolute culinary delight.

Ingredients

  • Dosa batter
  • Spiced potato stuffing
  • Red chili-garlic paste
  • Ghee or oil
  • Fresh butter
  • Sambar
  • Coconut chutney
  • Tomato chutney

Steps

  1. Heat a non-stick tawa or cast-iron skillet and lightly grease it with oil or ghee.
  2. Pour a ladleful of dosa batter into the center, spreading it evenly in a thin circular motion.
  3. Spread a thin layer of the reddish chili-garlic paste across the inner surface of the dosa.
  4. Drizzle ghee around the edges and cook on medium heat until the bottom turns golden and crisp.
  5. Place a generous scoop of the spiced potato filling in the center and fold the dosa over.
  6. Serve hot on a banana leaf with a dollop of butter, alongside sambar and fresh chutneys.
