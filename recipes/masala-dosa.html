Masala Dosa

This authentic South Indian Masala Dosa features thin, crispy rice-and-lentil crepes filled with a flavorful, spiced potato stuffing and a signature fiery red chutney spread. Traditionally served on vibrant banana leaves, this classic dish brings a perfect blend of a shattering, golden crust and a soft, deeply savory interior.

Ingredients

  • Dosa batter
  • Spiced potato stuffing
  • Red chili-garlic paste
  • Ghee or oil
  • Fresh butter
  • Sambar
  • Coconut chutney
  • Tomato chutney

Steps

  • Heat a non-stick tawa or cast-iron skillet and lightly grease it with oil or ghee.
  • Pour a ladleful of dosa batter into the center, spreading it evenly in a thin circular motion.
  • Spread a thin layer of the reddish chili-garlic paste across the inner surface of the dosa.
  • Drizzle ghee around the edges and cook on medium heat until the bottom turns golden and crisp.
  • Place a generous scoop of the spiced potato filling in the center and fold the dosa over.
  • Serve hot on a banana leaf with a dollop of butter, alongside sambar and fresh chutneys.
