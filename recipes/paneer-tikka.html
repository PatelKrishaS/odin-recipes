Paneer Tikka

This restaurant-style Paneer Tikka features succulent paneer cubes, crisp bell peppers, and onions marinated in a spiced yogurt blend and grilled to smoky perfection

Ingredients

  • Paneer cubes
  • Bell pepper squares
  • Onion layers
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Tikka spices (chili, coriander, garam masala, chaat masala, kasuri methi)
  • Oil and salt
  • Onion slices and lime wedges
  • Green chutney
  • Fresh cilantro

Steps

  • Whisk the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, oil, and all spices together in a large bowl.
  • Gently fold the paneer cubes, bell peppers, and onions into the marinade until evenly coated.
  • Cover and refrigerate the mixture for 30 minutes to let the flavours develop.
  • Thread the marinated paneer and vegetables alternately onto three skewers.
  • Grill on a preheated tawa or oven until the edges turn golden-brown and slightly charred.
  • Place the skewers on a plate, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot with onions, lime, and chutney.
