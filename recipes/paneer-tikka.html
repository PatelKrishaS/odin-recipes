Paneer Tikka

Description

This restaurant-style Paneer Tikka features succulent paneer cubes, crisp bell peppers, and onions marinated in a spiced yogurt blend and grilled to smoky perfection

Arranged beautifully on three skewers, the charred, golden-brown bites are served on a platter alongside crisp onion slices, juicy lime wedges, and a vibrant green chutney. A final flourish of fresh cilantro adds a burst of color and herb flavor, making it an irresistible appetizer for any occasion

Ingredients

  • Paneer cubes
  • Bell pepper squares
  • Onion layers
  • Yogurt
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Tikka spices (chili, coriander, garam masala, chaat masala, kasuri methi)
  • Oil and salt
  • Onion slices and lime wedges
  • Green chutney
  • Fresh cilantro

Steps

  1. Whisk the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, oil, and all spices together in a large bowl.
  2. Gently fold the paneer cubes, bell peppers, and onions into the marinade until evenly coated.
  3. Cover and refrigerate the mixture for 30 minutes to let the flavours develop.
  4. Thread the marinated paneer and vegetables alternately onto three skewers.
  5. Grill on a preheated tawa or oven until the edges turn golden-brown and slightly charred.
  6. Place the skewers on a plate, garnish with cilantro, and serve hot with onions, lime, and chutney.
